Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version: your music, images and videos never leave your device. Afterhours has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The only personal data involved is what is needed to host the website, sell the desktop app and answer your emails.

Who is responsible

Afterhours is made by Oscar, an independent developer in Oslo, Norway (“I”, “me”). I am responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: afterhours.hyper.void@gmail.com.

Your music, images and videos

Everything you load into Afterhours – songs, images, GIFs, videos, lyrics and text – is processed only on your own device, in your browser or in the desktop app. It is never uploaded to me or anyone else. Videos you export are saved directly to your device.

Live audio (system sound or microphone) is only used while you turn it on, and is analysed on your device. It is not recorded or sent anywhere, except when you yourself start a live export to a file on your device.

Settings stored on your device

The app remembers your settings, presets and texts in your browser’s local storage (or in the desktop app’s own folder). This data stays on your device, is only used to make the app work the way you left it, and is not a tracking cookie. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data or deleting the desktop app.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like all web hosts, GitHub receives technical data such as your IP address when you visit, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Fonts and code libraries are served from the website itself, not from third-party services.

Buying the desktop app (Gumroad)

The desktop app is sold through Gumroad, which acts as the seller (merchant of record) and handles payment, tax and receipts. Your card details go to Gumroad and its payment providers, never to me. Gumroad shares your name, email address, country and purchase details with me so I can deliver your license and give you support. See Gumroad’s privacy policy.

License activation

When you activate the desktop app, it sends your license key to Gumroad to check that it is valid. It checks again now and then (about every two weeks) so refunded purchases can be locked. Gumroad sees your IP address when this happens. The app does not send anything else.

Auto-detect lyrics (desktop app)

The first time you use auto-detect lyrics, the app downloads a speech-recognition model and code from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. These services see your IP address when the files are downloaded. The listening itself happens on your computer – your song is never uploaded.

Donations (PayPal)

If you choose to support Afterhours through PayPal, the payment is handled by PayPal under PayPal’s privacy statement. I see your name, email and the amount.

Email

If you email me, I use your email address and what you write only to answer you and help with your purchase.

Why I may use this data (legal basis)

  • To deliver what you bought (license, downloads, refunds, support) – contract.
  • To keep the website and licenses secure and stop key sharing – legitimate interest.
  • To keep sales records where the law requires it – legal obligation.

I do not sell your data, use it for advertising or send newsletters without asking.

How long I keep it

Purchase and support information is kept for as long as you may need help with your license, and for as long as accounting rules require. Emails are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I have about you, or object to how it is used. Just email me. If you think I am handling your data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or the authority in your own country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
